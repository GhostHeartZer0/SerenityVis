Because you already split your architecture cleanly between a C++ DSP backend and a WebGPU frontend, the migration path is straightforward. Here is the blueprint for moving your 5-phase engine to Android.

### 1. The Audio Backend (DSP & C++)

You must rip out `AudioServer.cpp`'s reliance on `mmdeviceapi.h` and Windows Sockets.

* **Capture Engine:** Replace WASAPI with **Oboe** (Google’s C++ wrapper for AAudio/OpenSL ES). It provides the lowest possible latency on Android.

You will need to rely primarily on the device microphone (`MediaRecorder.AudioSource.UNPROCESSED`) to drive the fluid physics.

As of 2026, the Android WebView engine fully supports WebGPU. You can inject your JNI audio data directly into the DOM using `WebView.evaluateJavascript`.

Your "Phase 5: Silicon" tasks require mapping Windows desktop APIs to Android equivalents to keep the app hardware-aware and efficient.
